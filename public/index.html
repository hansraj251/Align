About AlignOS

Built for Modern Restaurants

AlignOS is a Local-based Restaurant Management Software designed for cafés, restaurants, kitchens and hotels. From billing and kitchen management to inventory, staff management and business reports, everything is available from one simple dashboard.

Features

Everything You Need To Run Your Restaurant

Powerful tools designed to simplify restaurant operations.

📍

Areas & Tables

Create multiple dining areas and arrange tables across multiple rows. Customize each area with six color options and view tables from all areas together on one screen.

🔒

Table Lock & Reservation

Lock tables when they are unavailable and reserve tables in advance to manage seating and table availability efficiently.

🗂️

Menu Categories

Start with built-in menu categories and add as many additional categories as your restaurant needs.

🍽️

Menu Management

Add menu items, assign them to categories, and choose from six food type options to organize your menu clearly.

🔎

Quick Menu Search

Quickly find menu items while taking orders using simple keyword-based search, making order entry faster and easier.

➕

Item Variants

Add variants to menu items for different serving options such as Full, Half, and other available portions or variations.

🔄

Item Availability

Quickly mark menu items as Available or Not Available with a simple toggle whenever an item is temporarily unavailable.

🥤

Quick Items

Add quick-sale items such as water bottles and other items that do not require kitchen preparation. Quick Items are included directly in the bill without being sent to the KOT.

💳

POS Billing

Fast GST billing with multiple payment methods.

💵

Cashier Dashboard

Instantly view ready orders, generate bills and complete customer payments with ease.

👥

Staff Management

Add and manage staff by role, including Waiter, Manager, Cashier, Kitchen and Device. Assign role-based access according to your restaurant's operational requirements.

📱

Common Order Device

Use the Device role like a waiter account for a shared ordering device, allowing common order-taking access without assigning the device to an individual waiter.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Give staff access according to their role and responsibilities, helping keep operational functions available only where needed.

📋

Staff Details

Maintain essential staff information including name, mobile number, joining date and basic salary.

✅

Active & Inactive Staff

Easily mark staff as Active or Inactive to control their availability without removing their staff record.

📊

Reports & Analytics

View sales, taxes, expenses and business insights.

🖨️

Print KOT

Print KOTs to multiple printers and route them area-wise according to your kitchen and restaurant setup.

🍳

Kitchen Display System

Manage kitchen orders with Start Preparing and Ready status tracking for a clear and efficient kitchen workflow.

📺

Multi-Kitchen KDS

Manage multiple kitchens with area-wise filtering and track orders through Start Preparing and Ready statuses.

👀

View-Only Kitchen

Display KOTs on a kitchen screen without Start Preparing or Ready actions for simple and easy kitchen operations.

📱

Android App

Download the Align Android app directly from the Align website, install it on your Android device and log in using your Align account.

💻

Windows App

Download and install the Align Windows application directly from the Align website and log in to start using your POS on a local PC.

🔗

Easy Device Connection

Once your POS is running, open the Settings page to display the connection QR code. Scan the QR code from the Align Android app to connect the device to your POS.

📶

Same Network Connection

Connect Android devices to your local POS easily over the same Wi-Fi or local network. No separate cloud database connection is required for local device communication.

🔑

Align Account Login

Create your Align account at alignos.in first, then use your account credentials to log in to the installed Align applications and access your POS setup.

🖨️

Multi-Printer Setup

Add multiple USB or network printers from the Settings page and configure your restaurant's printing setup in one place.

🔀

Smart Print Routing

Route bills and KOTs to different printers according to your restaurant setup, including area-wise printing for multiple dining and kitchen areas.

💳

Subscription Management

View your current plan, subscription status, plan start and end dates, and remaining subscription days in one place.

📅

Plan & Expiry Details

Quickly check your active subscription period, expiry date, and remaining days so you know when your plan needs to be renewed.

📱

Device Management

See the number of devices allowed under your subscription and view currently active devices connected to your Align account.

🚪

Active Device Logout

View active devices and log out individual devices remotely when access is no longer required or a device needs to be disconnected.

📊

Sales Reports

View detailed sales summaries for any selected date range and get a clear overview of your restaurant's sales performance.

🧾

Orders Report

Review orders for a selected date range and keep track of your restaurant's order activity and operations.

🍽️

Item Sales Report

See which menu items are being sold and analyze item-level sales over your selected reporting period.

💳

Payment Reports

Review payment activity and analyze payments received during the selected date range.

📅

Date Range Reports

Select a From and To date to generate reports for exactly the period you want to analyze.

📥

Excel & PDF Export

Download your reports in Excel or PDF format for record keeping, analysis, printing, and sharing.

💱

Currency Settings

Choose from five supported currencies and configure the currency used across your restaurant's billing and POS operations.

🌍

Time Zone Settings

Select the appropriate time zone for your restaurant so dates, times, orders, reports and other time-based records follow your selected local time.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is AlignOS Cloud based?

No. Your restaurant data is securely stored in the Local and can be accessed from Local Devices.

Can I use AlignOS on multiple devices?

Yes. Owners, managers, cashiers, waiters and kitchen staff can use different devices simultaneously.

Does AlignOS support GST billing?

Yes. GST billing and tax reports are fully supported.
